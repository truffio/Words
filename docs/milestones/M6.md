## Summary

The game now has three rooms. The child first chooses a room from pictures of each one, then an activity. After a round, the summary offers *Play again*, *Change activity*, and *Change room*. Only the open room is kept in memory: leaving a room frees its textures and data, and the next room in the list is downloaded in the background so that opening it is quick.

Adding a room is now one command, `npm run new-room <id> "<Title>"`. It starts a **draft** room, which is checked but never built or shown, so an unfinished room can't break `dev`, CI, or deployment.

The two new rooms, a bedroom and a bathroom, are **placeholders drawn by a script**, because paintings can't be generated in this workflow. They work exactly like painted rooms and will be replaced with paintings in the style guide's style.

M6 was built before M5 (iPad), so there are no iPad measurements yet.

## What Was Built

### Room selection (#56)

- The first screen lists every room as a large button with a picture and its name. `build:rooms` now writes a 480 × 360 `thumbnail.webp` for each room.
- After choosing a room, the activity screen shows the room's name, the three activities, and *Change room*. The room is visible behind it.
- The round summary now has three buttons: *Play again*, *Change activity*, and *Change room*.
- Choosing the room that is already open shows its activities straight away, without loading it again.

### Game menu (#71)

- A menu icon (☰) sits in the top-right corner of the game window whenever a room is open: on the activity choice, during a round, and on the round summary.
- It opens a menu (a modal dialog) with **Restart activity**, **Change activity**, **Change room**, and **Back to the game**. *Restart activity* appears only once an activity has been played in the room.
- **Opening the menu pauses the round.** The clock stops (`session.pause()` / `resume()`; paused time doesn't count), and a challenge that has just ended waits to move on until the menu closes. The room and the HUD can't be used behind the menu.
- Closing it with *Back to the game* or Escape returns to the same challenge. In Recall and spell, the cursor goes back into the spelling box with the text the child had typed.
- The menu takes keyboard focus. Escape closes it.

### Loading and unloading (#57)

- **One room at a time.** When `RoomScene` shuts down, it removes every texture keyed `<roomId>:…`: the background, the cut-out atlas, the feedback overlays, and the debug overlays. It also removes the room's cached layout and hit map. The browser's HTTP cache keeps the files, so returning to a room doesn't download them again.
- **Next room prefetched.** Two seconds after a room opens, `src/game/prefetch.js` downloads the next room's files at low priority into the HTTP cache, without decoding them. Failures are ignored.
- **Loading indicator.** The loading screen has a progress bar fed by Phaser's loader (`roomLoadProgress` event).
- **Shared assets:** `Boot` is where they will be preloaded once. There are none yet; feedback sounds come with M3.

### Draft rooms and `new-room` (#58)

- `npm run new-room <id> "<Title>"` (`tools/new-room.js`) checks the id and creates `content/rooms/<id>/` with a starter manifest marked `"draft": true`. It then prints the next steps.
- A **draft room** is fully checked by `validate:content` and `build:rooms`, but every problem it has is reported as a warning marked *(draft)*. It isn't built and isn't shown in the game, so it never blocks `dev`, CI, or deployment. To publish it, remove `"draft": true`.
- The style guide's workflow ([§4](../art/style-guide.md#4-workflow)) now starts with `new-room` and ends by removing `"draft"`.

### Placeholder rooms (#59)

- `npm run make:placeholder-rooms` (`tools/make-placeholder-rooms.js`) draws the bedroom and the bathroom with their masks. The drawing helpers moved to `tools/lib/placeholder.js`, which the M1 kitchen script now uses too. The refactored kitchen script produces byte-identical files to the M1 placeholder.
- **Bedroom:** bed, pillow, lamp, wardrobe, desk, book, chair, window, clock, rug, teddy bear.
- **Bathroom:** sink, toilet, bathtub, shower, mirror, towel, toothbrush, soap, window, bath mat.
- They reuse the shared words *window*, *chair*, *clock*, and *sink*, and add 16 new words to the lexicon, with variants such as *closet*, *carpet*, *teddy*, *bath*, *tub*, and *mat*.
- `make:placeholder-kitchen` now takes an output folder. Without one, it still overwrites `content/rooms/kitchen`, which now holds the real painting; the README says so.

### Room list (#61)

[docs/rooms.md](../rooms.md) proposes 12 rooms of 25 words each: kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room, classroom, music room, playground, garden, grocery store, farm, beach, and street. That is 300 entries but only 227 different words, because shared words such as *chair* (5 rooms) and *window* (5 rooms) are written once. It also notes spelling variants, the need for an outdoor version of the art style, and animals as an exception to *no faces*.

### Tests (#62)

- **Unit tests** (131 in all, 9 new): draft rooms (problems become warnings, a complete draft is kept apart from the rooms to build, non-drafts still fail) and `new-room` (argument checks, starter manifest).
- **Test bridge:** `state.room` and `textureKeys()`.
- **End-to-end tests** (16 in all, 3 new in `rooms.spec.js`):
  - Choose the bedroom, play a full round where every target is a bedroom word (checked against the manifest file), change room to the bathroom, and play. The targets, the word choices, and the tap points then all belong to the bathroom.
  - The activity screen leads back to the rooms, and choosing the open room again shows its activities.
  - Six switches between the three rooms: after each one, only the open room's textures are in memory, the second visit holds no more than the first, and there are no page errors.
- The existing tests now choose the kitchen first.

## Performance

Measured on the development laptop, desktop Chromium, served by `vite preview` (test build), 1280 × 800:

| Measure | Result | Budget (§6) |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Open a room, first time (cold, local server) | 0.44–0.65 s | ≤ 3 s on Wi-Fi |
| Open a room again (cached) | 0.59–0.66 s | ≤ 1 s |
| Tap to feedback (pointer down → feedback text painted) | 1–6 ms | ≤ 100 ms |
| JavaScript heap after each of 12 switches (after garbage collection) | 15.4 MB every time | no growth |

| Room | Background | Atlas | Hit map | Atlas pages |
|------|-----------|-------|---------|-------------|
| Kitchen (painted) | 294 KB | 288 KB | 25 KB | 2025 × 1710, 2035 × 1251 |
| Bedroom (placeholder) | 36 KB | 80 KB | 18 KB | 2020 × 1898, 1994 × 482 |
| Bathroom (placeholder) | 32 KB | 60 KB | 14 KB | 1944 × 1482 |

All files are within budget. **Decoded texture memory** matters more on the iPad than file sizes. The kitchen needs about 12.6 MB for its background and about 24 MB for its two atlas pages (cut-outs plus glow halos), about 37 MB in all. Only one room is in memory at a time, so this should be fine. Check it in Safari Web Inspector in #60.

Desktop numbers on a local server say little about an iPad on Wi-Fi. #60 stays open for the real measurements.

## Deviations from the Plan

- **Placeholder rooms instead of paintings** (#59), as agreed: paintings can't be generated in this workflow. Replacing a placeholder means loading its painting in the mask tool and remaking the masks; the manifest and lexicon stay.
- **The placeholder rooms have 10–11 words**, not ~25. The full lists are in rooms.md for the paintings.
- **No iPad measurements** (#60): M5 hasn't been done, and the iPad has to be measured by hand.
- **Room order** is alphabetical by folder name (bathroom, bedroom, kitchen), not the order in rooms.md. An `order` field in the manifest can fix this when there are more rooms.
- **No *Change room* during a round.** As #56 asks, the room screen is reached from the start screen and the round summary.
